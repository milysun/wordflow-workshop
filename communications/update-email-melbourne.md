**Subject:** Monday's Wordflow workshop: one quick update before you come

---

Hi all,

We're looking forward to seeing you on **Monday 26 October, 2:00-5:00 pm**, in **Meeting Room M03, Mezzanine, Melbourne Connect**, for *Point-and-click text analytics: an introduction to LDaCA Wordflow*.

A new version of Wordflow came out this week, with a fix that matters for the afternoon. **Please update before Monday**:

- **Already installed Wordflow?** Open it. It offers the update when it starts: accept it, and it's done in a minute. (No prompt? Quit and reopen the app, or choose **Help → Check for Updates**.)
- **Not installed yet?** Go to **sih.tools/wordflow** and install the desktop app for Mac or Windows (about five minutes).
  - **Mac**: at the "downloaded from the internet" prompt, click **Open**.
  - **Windows**: if a security warning appears, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Can't install software on your laptop?** The same page has a browser option, **Launch in Binder**, which needs your university sign-in and a couple of minutes to start. You can also just come along: we'll make sure you can follow.

**What to bring:** your laptop and its charger. That's all: the afternoon uses Wordflow's built-in example data, and access to an AI model for the last part is provided on the day (if you have your own OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic or Google API key, you're welcome to use it). If you have a text dataset from your own research, bring it too: the last half hour is open time to try Wordflow on it with us beside you.

**How the afternoon works:** it's hands-on throughout. We show each step briefly, do it together, then leave time for you to explore on your own, with helpers in the room. No programming experience is needed.
