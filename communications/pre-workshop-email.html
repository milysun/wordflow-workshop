Subject: Wordflow workshop — what to bring (and what NOT to bring)

Hi all,

Looking forward to seeing you on this coming Wednesday for the Wordflow workshop. This email has everything you need to be ready.

What is Wordflow, briefly?

Wordflow is a web-based tool for text analytics — concordance, frequency, topic modelling, comparative analysis — without writing code. It's part of the LDaCA Text Analytics Tools suite. You'll be able to use it through a browser, or completely locally if you like to install the app on your computer.

What to bring

  • Your laptop (any operating system; a recent browser).
  • Power cable — the venue has some power outlets in case your battery complains.
  • Curiosity about a corpus you'd like to explore. You don't need to bring data, but if you have a small text corpus you're curious about, that opens up an optional "bring your own data" track at the end.

What NOT to bring

  • Don't bring sensitive or unpublished data for the workshop's cloud-hosted version. Anything you upload during the workshop is uploaded to a temporary cloud server on Nectar. It's deleted after your session, but for sensitive data it's recommended to use the local install — the desktop installer is on sih.tools/wordflow (see the optional pre-install section below).
  • Don't bring Word documents or PDFs as your corpus — they won't import directly. If you have them, convert to CSV with at least one text column, or a bunch of plain txt files in a zip file.

Format expectations if you bring data

If you'd like to try the "bring your own data" track:

  • CSV (UTF-8 encoding, header row, one row per document) — most common.
  • Excel also works (single sheet).
  • Size: under 50 MB and under 100,000 rows for a comfortable workshop experience. If you have more, bring a sample or the embedded sampling tool can help.

If your data doesn't fit those constraints, no problem — there are great sample datasets we'll use together, and we'll have an "open lab" segment where you can choose any of three exploration tracks.

What we WON'T cover

This is a 3-hour intro. We won't be diving deep into every tool, parameter, or advanced workflow. We aim for confident beginner, not power user. Everything you learn will transfer to the rest of the toolkit, and the documentation will be linked in the follow-up email.

Optional but recommended — install the desktop app as a backup

Takes about 5 minutes. If Binder works fine on the day, you've still got a useful tool installed for later.
