**Point-and-click text analytics: Wordflow comes to Melbourne, 26 October**

Our first workshop outside Sydney! Join the Melbourne Data Analytics Platform (MDAP), the Language Data Commons of Australia (LDaCA) and the Sydney Informatics Hub for a free, hands-on introduction to **LDaCA Wordflow**, the point-and-click text analysis app we develop with the Sydney Corpus Lab. If you work with text (interviews, social media, news, speeches, survey comments) but not with Python or R, this session is for you: follow a real research workflow built live, from a raw dataset to a small finding, then try Wordflow's new GenAI **Annotation** tool, writing a codebook, coding a small sample yourself, and checking how well an AI model agrees with you before letting it code more. **Monday 26 October, 2:00-5:00 pm, Meeting Room M03, Mezzanine, Melbourne Connect, University of Melbourne.** Open to researchers from all disciplines and universities, HDR students especially welcome; no programming experience needed; bring your own laptop. This one is **in-person in Melbourne only**, so if you have colleagues or collaborators there, please pass it along. [Register free](https://rduevents.unimelb.edu.au/event/2814-point-and-click-text-analytics-an-introduction-to-ldaca).
