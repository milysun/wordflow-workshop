Hi all,

Looking forward to seeing you on **[DATE]** for the **Wordflow** workshop. This email has everything you need to be ready.

## What is Wordflow, briefly?

Wordflow is a web-based tool for text analytics — concordance, frequency, topic modelling, comparative analysis — without writing code. It's part of the LDaCA Text Analytics Tools suite. You'll be using it through a browser; no installation required for the workshop.

## What to bring

- **Your laptop** (any operating system; a recent browser).
- **Power cable** — the venue has outlets but the workshop is long.
- **Curiosity about a corpus you'd like to explore.** You don't need to bring data, but if you have a small text corpus you're curious about, that opens up an optional "bring your own data" track at the end.

## What NOT to bring

- **Don't bring sensitive or unpublished data** for the workshop's cloud-hosted version. Anything you upload during the workshop is uploaded to a temporary cloud server. It's deleted after your session, but for sensitive data you should use the local install — the desktop installer is on **bit.ly/wordflows** (see the optional pre-install section below).
- **Don't bring Word documents or PDFs** as your corpus — they won't import directly. If you have them, convert to CSV with at least one text column first. (Tools we can recommend: spreadsheet copy-paste for small corpora, Pandoc for batch conversion, or LDaCA's ingestion tools for serious work.)

## Format expectations if you bring data

If you'd like to try the "bring your own data" track:

- **CSV** (UTF-8 encoding, header row, one row per document) — most common.
- **Excel** also works (single sheet).
- **Size**: under 50 MB and under 100,000 rows for a comfortable workshop experience. If you have more, bring a sample.

If your data doesn't fit those constraints, no problem — there are great sample datasets we'll use together, and we'll have an "open lab" segment where you can choose any of three exploration tracks.

## What we'll cover

- **0:00 – 0:25** — intro to the project, team, and the Wordflow interface.
- **0:25 – 0:55** — snapshot tour: touch all five analytic tools in 30 minutes.
- **0:55 – 1:10** — break.
- **1:10 – 1:55** — a real research workflow on QLD election tweets, end to end.
- **1:55 – 2:10** — break.
- **2:10 – 2:25** — the lens-repurpose demo (the most quotable 15 minutes).
- **2:25 – 2:55** — free hands-on lab with three tracks: continue Session 2 / your own data / open exploration.
- **2:55 – 3:00** — wrap-up.

**A note on Session 2:** it's a guided demo with selective follow-along. You don't have to follow every click. We'll provide pre-built checkpoint files (snapshots and workspace archives) so anyone who falls behind can rejoin in 30 seconds.

## What we WON'T cover

This is a 3-hour intro. We won't be diving deep into every tool, parameter, or advanced workflow. We aim for **confident beginner**, not power user. Everything you learn will transfer to the rest of the toolkit, and the documentation will be linked in the follow-up email.

Takes about 5 minutes. If Binder works fine on the day, you've still got a useful tool installed for later.
