Hi all,

Thank you for registering for Wednesday's workshops on the Proceedings of Federal Parliament. Many of you are coming to both sessions, so this one email covers both; your Eventbrite ticket shows which you've registered for.

Both sessions are in the **Western Tower Boardroom** (room S495, level 4), The Quadrangle (A14), The University of Sydney:

- **9:15-10:45 am: Getting started with the Proceedings of Federal Parliament in your research.** Please **arrive by 9:00** so we can start on time.
- **11:00 am-12:30 pm: Analysing parliamentary discourse at scale with the new Wordflow app.**

The two are designed to run in a row. The first shows where the debates come from and how to find the ones you need; the second takes thousands of them at once into Wordflow, a free point-and-click app, to trace how language shifts across parliaments and decades. **Registered for only one?** You're very welcome at the other too: register for [the 9:15 session](https://www.eventbrite.com.au/e/1999369770806/) or [the 11:00 session](https://www.eventbrite.com.au/e/1999370488954/) so we know to expect you.

For both sessions, **bring your laptop**, charged, and make sure it can get online. eduroam is available across campus.

## For the 11:00 session: set up Wordflow before Wednesday (5 minutes)

You'll be working along with us in Wordflow, so please have it ready before you arrive. Go to **[sih.tools/wordflow](https://sih.tools/wordflow)**, where there are two ways to run it:

**1. Install the desktop app (recommended).** Download the latest version for Mac or Windows and install it. It runs faster, and everything stays on your own computer, which matters once you start working with your own data.

- **macOS**: at the "downloaded from the internet" prompt, click **Open**.
- **Windows**: the installer may show a security warning; choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Already have Wordflow installed?** Open it and accept the **update notification**; the upgrade is one click. If no notification appears, quit and reopen the app.

Open the app once after installing to check it starts.

**2. Or run it in your browser.** If you can't install software (a university-managed laptop, for example), use the **Launch in Binder** option on the same page. It runs on ARDC's research cloud, needs an Australian or New Zealand university sign-in, and takes a couple of minutes to start, so launch it as you sit down. It works well for the workshop; for your own data later, we recommend the desktop app.

Stuck on either? Reply to this email, or tell us in the break between sessions and we'll help you get going.
