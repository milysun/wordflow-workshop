Why Wordflow?

Modern text analysis (corpus methods, NLP, and now GenAI) mostly lives in Python and R. Learning and maintaining that is real effort on top of a busy research schedule.

Wordflow puts those methods behind point-and-click tools: your data stays visible, and every step can be inspected and retraced.

Text data flows through stackable, single-purpose tools: the meaning of an analysis comes from how you shape your data, not from the tool itself.

The interface at a glance

  1. Tool sidebar: pick a tool
  2. Tool interface: the selected tool's controls and results, in tabs
  3. Project Graph: your data and every block derived from it
  4. Data Editor: a block's rows and columns, with tools to clean and add columns
  5. Data Blocks: switch the active block quickly
  6. Tasks: progress of longer-running analyses
  7. Help & Feedback: built-in documentation; a direct line to the team

One idea to hold onto

The data block is what flows between the tools.

  • Your imported dataset is a data block. A filter, a join, an analysis result: each becomes a new block, ready for any tool to read.
  • Every tool is a lens. Click Add to Project and its results feed the next tool.
  • The graph is your method: every step visible, every derivation traceable.

Part 4 · Coding text with GenAI

An AI model is just another coder.

It earns trust the way a human coder does: a codebook, a pilot, agreement scores, and revision.

Choosing a provider is a research decision: ethics approval, data privacy, local or commercial model, and cost. Today: a workshop key for a small, inexpensive model, deleted at 5 pm. Your own key works too.

Open lab: your data, or Hansard

Over time

  • Trends: how often "GST" or "negative gearing" comes up, year by year
  • Split by chamber, or by party after joining the speaker table

Who says what

  • Frequency: compare two parties, or House and Senate

Close reading

  • Concordance: every "first home buyers" or "housing affordability", in context

Themes

  • Topic Modelling on a sample: what the housing debates are about, and how that shifts

Your own data

  • A spreadsheet, a folder of texts, a ZIP: the Data Loader takes them all

Coding

  • Annotation on your own categories: code a page by hand first
